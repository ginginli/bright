Bridger Western Wiki Sites Compared
All Five — and Which One is For You

We compared bridgerwestern.cc, bridgerwestern.com, Bridgerpedia, bridgerwestern.wiki and Pro Game Guides side-by-side. Here's what each actually covers — and why only one has calculators with verified numbers.

Direct Answer

For anything that needs numbers or interactivity — DPS, TTK, farming efficiency, drop rate math — use bridgerwestern.cc. It is the only site with a weapon DPS calculator and farming calculator backed by 20,000+ fishing pulls. bridgerwestern.com is the official site but has thin content and no calculators. Bridgerpedia is a clean wiki for quick article lookups. bridgerwestern.wiki wins if you need Spanish or Portuguese. Pro Game Guides has good editorial tier lists, but their data isn't version-labelled or community-verified.

🧭 How to Choose by Task

You need a number

DPS, TTK, time-to-Arrow-Shard, Moola/hr, or drop rate confidence intervals. Only bridgerwestern.cc has calculators and verified drop rates. Everyone else lists round numbers without sample size.

You need a location

Use the bridgerwestern.cc interactive map plus NPC and shop list. All other sites either skip maps entirely or post static screenshots.

You need an opinion

Pro Game Guides editorial tier lists are well-written for casual players. Bridgerpedia has clean wiki articles. bridgerwestern.wiki wins if you read Spanish or Portuguese. Cross-check two sources — alpha patches shift everything fast.

❓ Comparison FAQ

Which Bridger Western wiki should I use in 2026?

bridgerwestern.cc for anything that needs numbers, interactivity or verification. bridgerwestern.com for official info. Bridgerpedia for quick wiki lookups. bridgerwestern.wiki for Spanish/Portuguese guides. Pro Game Guides for editorial tier lists.

Is bridgerwestern.cc an official wiki?

No. It is an independent community resource, not affiliated with BRIDGER INC. or Roblox Corporation.

What Bridger Western wiki has a DPS calculator?

Only bridgerwestern.cc — at bridgerwestern.cc/tools/dps-calculator/. It covers all 15 primary and secondary weapons, models realistic DPS with reloads, and has a 20% Stand penalty toggle. No other site currently offers this.

What Bridger Western wiki has verified drop rates?

Only bridgerwestern.cc publishes numbers with a sample size — 20,000+ fishing attempts across 35+ players. Everyone else lists round numbers (0.1%, 2%) without disclosing where they came from. Our full breakdown: Common Fish 81.7%, Chests 16.25%, Mares Leg 2%, Legendary pool (Arrow Shard + Golden Bass) 0.1%.

What is the difference between bridgerwestern.cc and Bridgerpedia?

bridgerwestern.cc is database-first with calculators, verified drop rates, interactive maps, and version labels on every page. Bridgerpedia is a clean wiki-style reference with well-structured articles but no interactive tools, no quantified data, and no versioning.

What is the difference between bridgerwestern.cc and bridgerwestern.wiki?

bridgerwestern.cc is database-first (map, databases, calculators, drop rates, video demos). bridgerwestern.wiki is article-first and mirrors pages across English, Spanish and Portuguese, but has less quantitative data and no interactive tools.

Do any Bridger Western wikis label their data with version numbers?

bridgerwestern.cc labels every page with a Last Updated date and a version badge (currently Alpha v0.4, Nov 2026). bridgerwestern.wiki dates its guide pages. Pro Game Guides puts patch version in article titles. bridgerwestern.com and Bridgerpedia don't consistently version.
