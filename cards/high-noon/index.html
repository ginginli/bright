Bridger Western High Noon Card – Tarot Card Guide 2026

The Bridger Western High Noon card is a High Risk Tarot Card. Cost: −10 Max HP. When the time reaches noon, it plays an audio cue and grants autoaim for 5 seconds. The High Noon Sniper build pairs it with EXECUTIONER and Quick Draw. See the All Cards database for the full card list and the Card Builds guide for all build combinations.
