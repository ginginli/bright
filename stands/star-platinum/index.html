Quick Answer

Star Platinum is a RARE time-manipulating Stand in Bridger Western. It is obtained through Arrow Shard fishing (0.1% drop rate) or Corpse Part KOTH events (15–20% success rate), and any obtainment roll has a 5% chance to grant a Shiny skin. Its signature move is V — Beatdown (11 | 2.4/punch | 14 dmg; grabs and pummels the target).

RARE HUMANOID Time-Manipulating

✨ Evolution

Using the G (Time Stop Movement) ability has a 1% chance to evolve Star Platinum into Star Platinum | The World.

🎯 How to Obtain

Stand rolls are random — you cannot target this Stand specifically. Every Arrow Shard or Corpse Part roll can result in any obtainable Stand — any obtainment roll has a 5% chance to grant a Shiny skin.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does Star Platinum evolve?

Yes — using the G (Time Stop Movement) ability has a 1% chance to evolve Star Platinum into Star Platinum | The World.

Can I target the evolution?

No. Evolution is a 1% chance, so you just have to keep using G on Star Platinum and hope for the best.

Is Star Platinum time-manipulating?

Yes — it is classified as RARE / HUMANOID / Time-Manipulating, alongside The World, King Crimson, TWAU, Mandom, and Whitesnake.

Does this Stand have a shiny variant?

Yes — any obtainment roll has a 5% chance to grant a Shiny skin. Check the Skins section on this page for confirmed variants.
