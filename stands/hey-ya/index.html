👋 What is Hey Ya?

👋
DEFAULT NON-HUMANOID Utility

A utility companion that provides buffs to the player without requiring active ability inputs.

⚡ Abilities & Stats

Passive

Summoning this stand takes no stamina.

This Stand has no active moves — all benefits come from its passives.

🎯 How to Obtain

Stand rolls are random — you cannot target this Stand specifically. Every Arrow Shard or Corpse Part roll can result in any obtainable Stand. Any obtainment roll has a 5% chance to grant a Shiny skin.

Method Requirement Rate
Arrow Shard fishing Fish up an Arrow Shard (rare fishing drop), then use it 0.1% drop rate
Corpse Part KOTH event 3+ players in server; grab the part and hold it (water method recommended) 15–20% success rate

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does this Stand have any active moves?

No — this Stand has no active moves at all. All benefits come from its passives.

What buffs does it give?

It supplies random buffs including Dodges, Damage buffs, Healing, Speed buffs, and Increased accuracy.

What is the utility stand good for?

It shines as a support/utility companion — it costs no stamina to summon and provides consistent random buffs.
