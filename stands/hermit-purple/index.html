Quick Answer

Hermit Purple is a DEFAULT NON-HUMANOID Stand in Bridger Western. It is obtained through Arrow Shard fishing (0.1% drop rate) or Corpse Part KOTH events (15–20% success rate), and any obtainment roll has a 5% chance to grant a Shiny skin. Its signature move is R — Chop (10 dmg; if used after landing E on a player, performs a dropkick).

🎯 How to Obtain

Stand rolls are random — you cannot target this Stand specifically. Every Arrow Shard or Corpse Part roll can result in any obtainable Stand. Any obtainment roll has a 5% chance to grant a Shiny skin.

Method Requirement Rate
Arrow Shard fishing Fish up an Arrow Shard (rare fishing drop), then use it 0.1% drop rate
Corpse Part KOTH event 3+ players in server; grab the part and hold it (water method recommended) 15–20% success rate

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I target this Stand specifically?

Stand rolls are random — you cannot target this Stand specifically. Every Arrow Shard or Corpse Part roll can result in any obtainable Stand.

What is the fastest way to get this Stand?

Corpse Part KOTH events are the fastest grind for any Rare Stand, with a 15–20% success rate, vs. Arrow Shard fishing which has a 0.1% drop rate.

Does this Stand have a shiny variant?

Yes — any obtainment roll has a 5% chance to grant a Shiny skin. Check the Skins section on this page for confirmed variants.
