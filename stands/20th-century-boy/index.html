Quick Answer

20th Century Boy is a DEFAULT NON-HUMANOID Stand in Bridger Western. It is obtained through Arrow Shard fishing (0.1% drop rate) or Corpse Part KOTH events (15–20% success rate), and any obtainment roll has a 5% chance to grant a Shiny skin. Its signature move is V — Dynamite Strap (12 dmg per dynamite; pull loose dynamite and attach it to your body; Absolute Defense negates self-damage from the explosion; requires 6 dynamite to use).
