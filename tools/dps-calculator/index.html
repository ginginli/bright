📖 How to Use This Calculator

  • Stand Penalty: Having any Stand applies approximately a 20% penalty to base gun damage. The exact percentage varies by Stand, but 20% is the community-verified average.
  • Revolver Reloads: Revolver-type weapons (Taurus, Schofield, Whitney, Colt Ocelot) have inefficient individual reload — but once the cylinder is fully reloaded the speed is the same. We account for the wasteful reload in our realistic DPS figure.
  • Tommy Gun Spread: The high fire rate comes with heavy bullet spread — at mid range you'll land fewer shots than the raw DPS suggests. Actual effectiveness drops significantly past close range.
  • Mares Leg: 2% fishing drop, or 1,500 Moola repair from the Blacksmith (requires the fishing drop first). Fast firing, low reload penalty — excellent value.
