🧠 Probability Primer — The Gambler's Fallacy

Each fishing pull is independent. If you've already fished 500 times without an Arrow Shard, you're not"due" — your next pull still has exactly the same chance. However, the binomial distribution means that after 2,000 pulls you have a 86.5% chance of having seen at least one 0.1% drop. After 5,000 pulls: 99.3%.
