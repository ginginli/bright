Direct Answer

The best gun in Bridger Western is the Malcom .70 for long range — a scoped 4-bullet rifle with 50 damage headshots. The Winchester Repeater is the best all-round weapon (8 bullets, longer range, high fire rate), the Tommy Gun is the best close-range secondary once you reach Tier 3, and the Mares Leg is the best value pickup at a 2% fishing drop.

⚖️ The Two Decisions That Matter

Malcom .70 vs Winchester Repeater

Pick the Malcom .70 if you fight at distance and can land headshots — 50 damage per headshot ends fights fast, but you only get 4 bullets.

Pick the Winchester Repeater if you take messy mid-range fights. Eight bullets and a high fire rate forgive missed shots in a way the sniper cannot.

Guns or Stand?

Having a Stand applies a damage penalty to base gun damage. If your build is gun-first, a Stand actively costs you output. Decide early whether you are building around firearms or Stand abilities.

Until Tier 3 you cannot equip a primary and a secondary at the same time, so most early loadouts are one gun plus mobility.
