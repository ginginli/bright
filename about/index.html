About Bridger Western Wiki
Sources, Verification & Update Policy

An independent, community-run database for Bridger Western — built around verified drop rates, an interactive map and video walkthroughs.

In One Sentence

bridgerwestern.cc is an independent community wiki for the Roblox game Bridger Western, covering Stands, weapons, cards, items, fishing, horses and locations across 65+ pages, with a public interactive map and video-verified acquisition walkthroughs. It is not affiliated with the developer or Roblox.

🔍 How We Verify Data

Confirmed

Numbers reproduced in-game — drop rates such as the 0.1% Arrow Shard or the 2% Mares Leg, item properties, weapon bullet counts, and mechanics that can be tested directly.

Where the game publishes an official description, we quote it and label the source.

Community-Reported

Fields that cannot be reproduced alone — KOTH success rates, clan behaviour, spawn timings — are labelled as community reports rather than stated as fact.

If a value is disputed, we say so instead of picking one number and presenting it as certain.

🗓️ Update Policy

Pages are reviewed against the current 2026 Alpha build. Each guide carries a visible "last updated" date, and the structured data on every page carries a matching dateModified value.

When a patch changes drop rates, ability behaviour or weapon stats, the affected pages are revised first — tier lists and acquisition guides take priority over cosmetic pages.

🧾 Independence

This site is not affiliated with BRIDGER INC. or Roblox Corporation. It is a fan-made reference maintained by the community.

Some pages contain affiliate links (for example a ping-reduction service); those links are marked as sponsored and do not affect rankings or recommendations.
